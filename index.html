Who we are

One crew for the whole show

We offer a full stack of services to assist venues, musicians, houses of worship, businesses and residents. We specialize in live production, and we also do A/V work, tour and band management, rentals and backline, home theater work and consulting.

Instead of hiring one company for sound, another for backline, another for booking and another for video, you get it under one roof, from a Corpus Christi team that's already working the local stages.

Our mission is to help local talent, venues and creators expand their productions and events with carefully curated entertainment, sound support services, education, and logistics.Andrew Kaneubbe, Owner / Operator
